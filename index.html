I am an academic and researcher in ICT, currently teaching at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Port City International University. My research lies at the intersection of quantum machine learning and AI security — including backdoor detection and repair in hybrid quantum neural networks — alongside medical and explainable AI, IoT, and robotics. I hold an M.Sc. in ICT from Comilla University, where I worked as a Research Assistant at the Quantum Machine Learning Lab.
